# Shot-list bimetálico — reels MOTOR y PAUSA (Vertex/Veo)

Clips 3D **Lujo Clínico Bimetálico** (ver `GUIA_IDENTIDAD_VISUAL_IA.md §10`) para reemplazar los
de arcilla en los reels de prospección. Genera **3–4 clips por reel** (~5–6s c/u); yo los secuencio
a los beats de la VO. Cámara **lenta y estable** que revela; el reflejo especular del metal da la
micro-novedad (nada de cortes rápidos). Sin humanos, sin texto, sin orgánico, sin neón.

## Base (pega al inicio de cada prompt)
```
Hyper-realistic 3D, quiet-luxury high-end fintech aesthetic. Brushed titanium and frosted glass
materials, subtle anodized champagne-gold accents (#C5A059), deep carbon-fiber dark background
(#0F1115). Studio lighting with sharp specular highlights and ambient occlusion (contact shadows).
Vertical 9:16, cinematic depth of field, slow stable camera reveal. No humans, no text, no organic
textures, no neon.
```

---

## Reel MOTOR (remar vs. apalancamiento)

1. **Esfuerzo bruto** — *"a heavy dark-titanium mechanism being forced to turn by sheer brute
   pressure, straining, visible friction and resistance, slow grind, sparks of stress on the metal."*
2. **El motor se enciende (revelado clave)** — *"a bimetallic engine core ignites with a warm
   champagne-gold glow at its center and instantly powers the whole structure into effortless smooth
   rotation; the gold light spreads through the titanium, specular highlights sweeping across the
   bevels."* ← aquí cae "apalancamiento" + el SFX de arranque.
3. **La tecnología hace el trabajo pesado** — *"a precise automated bimetallic machine moving massive
   frosted-glass and titanium blocks effortlessly and frictionlessly, calm and unstoppable, gold
   accents pulsing in rhythm."*
4. **CTA / hero** — *"a single monolithic bimetallic block with a glowing gold core at rest, centered,
   clean negative space, one slow specular highlight sweeping across it."*

## Reel PAUSA (dependencia de su presencia)

1. **Todo depende de usted (frágil)** — *"a single glowing champagne-gold orb balancing precariously
   at the very top of a tall, impossibly thin titanium pillar; the whole structure trembles slightly;
   everything depends on that one point."*
2. **Si usted para, todo para** — *"an intricate bimetallic gear mechanism suddenly freezes and stops
   dead; hard cutting shadows fall; the gold light in the core fades as motion halts."*
3. **La cuerda floja** — *"a gold sphere balancing on a single taut, thin titanium wire stretched
   across deep darkness, tense and unstable, minimal, the wire vibrating."* ← aquí cae "cuerda floja"
   + el SFX de tensión.
4. **Sigue produciendo aunque usted no esté** — *"a bimetallic system continues to pulse, flow and
   produce autonomously with warm gold light circulating through its channels, calm and self-sustaining,
   no one present."*
5. **CTA / hero** — igual que MOTOR beat 4.
